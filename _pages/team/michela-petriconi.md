### [Michela Petriconi](https://petriconi.org/)

**PhD student**

Michela studies the theory of inverse reinforcement learning and inverse bandits. Her recent work asks what a demonstrator’s choices reveal about how the demonstrator estimates the gaps in reward between actions.

[Learning from a Bandit Learner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26103) (2026 preprint).
