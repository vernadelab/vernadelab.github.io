### [Amer Essakine](https://aessakine.github.io/)

**PhD student**

Amer’s PhD research explores how agents can represent and use memory in partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs). His earlier work on risk-sensitive reinforcement learning established tight bounds on how much interaction is needed to identify a near-optimal policy under an entropic risk measure.

[Tight Sample Complexity Bounds for Entropic Best Policy Identification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13717) (COLT 2026).
