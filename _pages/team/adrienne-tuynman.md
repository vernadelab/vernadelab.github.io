### [Adrienne Tuynman](https://adriennetuynman.github.io/)

**Postdoctoral researcher**

Adrienne studies sequential testing and pure exploration in bandits and reinforcement learning. Her recent work uses e-values to design tests that can stop collecting data once there is enough evidence.

[Optimal Posterior E-values with Non-Convex Parameter Sets with Applications to Voting Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29998) (2026 preprint).
