Claire Vernade gave a department seminar at the University of Technology Nuremberg titled “An Introduction to (Multi-Objective) Imitation Learning.” She presented joint work with Ziyad Sheebaelhamd, Luca Viano, and Volkan Cevher on learning from demonstrations by experts with different objectives.
