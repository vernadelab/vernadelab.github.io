### [Alexander von Rohr](https://avrohr.com/)

**Postdoctoral researcher**

Alexander works on Bayesian optimization and safe, robust reinforcement learning. His recent work develops methods for tuning reinforcement learning algorithms that account for both expected performance and variation across training runs.

[Efficient Heteroscedastic Bayesian Optimization for Risk-Aware AutoRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26680) (RLC 2026).
