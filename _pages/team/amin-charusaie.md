## Postdoctoral researchers

### [Amin Charusaie](https://charusaie.github.io/)

**Postdoctoral researcher**

Amin studies how machine learning systems and human experts can make decisions together. His work on learning to defer asks when a model should hand a decision to a human while meeting constraints such as fairness or a limited budget for expert review.

[A Unifying Post-Processing Framework for Multi-Objective Learn-to-Defer Problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12710) (NeurIPS 2024).
