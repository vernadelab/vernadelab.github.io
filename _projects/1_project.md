## ERC Starting Grant · 2025–2030

ConSequentIAL develops foundations for continual and sequential learning systems that can adapt to shifting data, seek information under uncertainty, and account for their own impact on the environment.

The project connects constrained and non-stationary reinforcement learning with active data acquisition and long-term skill planning.
