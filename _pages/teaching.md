## Lecture sequence

1. **Introduction to Reinforcement Learning (CM)**
2. **Dynamic Programming** — [Interactive HTML lecture](https://vernadelab.github.io/intro-rl/lecture-02-dynamic-programming/)
3. **Model-Free Prediction** — [Interactive HTML lecture](https://vernadelab.github.io/intro-rl/lecture-03-model-free-prediction/)
4. **Model-Free Control I (CM)** — [HTML lecture slides](https://download.cmutschler.de/lectures/UTN_RL_2026/04_Model-free_Control-v12.html#5)
5. **Model-Free Control II (CM)**
6. **Policy Gradient Methods I**
7. **Policy Gradient Methods II**
8. **Policy Gradient Methods III**
9. **Model-Based Reinforcement Learning I (CM)**
10. **Model-Based Reinforcement Learning II (CM)**
11. **Introduction to Exploration**
12. **Exploration in Deep RL and Modern RL Theory Questions**

Lectures marked **(CM)** are taught by [Christopher Mutschler](https://mlps-lab.de/christopher-mutschler/).

The course schedule also includes a guest lecture, with the topic to be announced.

- **Foundations of Reinforcement Learning and Control** — INFORMS tutorial given by [Onno Eberhard](https://onnoeberhard.com/) and [Martha White](https://apps.ualberta.ca/directory/person/whitem) (University of Alberta).
- **Contextual Bandits** — Reinforcement Learning Summer School, Barcelona, 2023. [Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfTUzEptKsI).
- **Foundations for Continual Reinforcement Learning** — EWRL keynote, 2024.
