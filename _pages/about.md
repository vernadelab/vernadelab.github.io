We study the mathematical foundations of **interactive and adaptive learning systems**. Our work spans reinforcement learning, bandit algorithms, continual learning, and learning theory.

Most learning theory assumes a fixed world and a single objective. We study what happens when the world changes, feedback arrives late or indirectly, and a learner must acquire useful knowledge over a lifetime.

The group is led by [Claire Vernade](https://www.cvernade.com/) and is part of the [University of Technology Nuremberg](https://www.utn.de/en/).

## Research areas

- **Continual and lifelong learning:** agents that reuse experience, adapt to change, and plan what to learn next.
- **Reinforcement learning theory:** principled algorithms for decision-making under uncertainty.
- **Bandit algorithms:** efficient exploration with structured, delayed, or partial feedback.
