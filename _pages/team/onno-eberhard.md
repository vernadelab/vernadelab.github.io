### [Onno Eberhard](https://onnoeberhard.com/)

**PhD student**

Onno studies how reinforcement learning agents can act when they observe only part of their environment. His recent work analyzes policies that act on the current observation alone and how recurrent memory can help when that observation is insufficient. Co-supervised with Michael Muehlebach.

[Foundations of Reinforcement Learning and Control: Connections and New Perspectives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02433) (INFORMS tutorial article, 2026).
